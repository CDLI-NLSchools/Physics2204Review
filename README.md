# Physics 2204 Review

A single-file practice app for Physics 2204 (Newfoundland and Labrador curriculum): generated practice questions with worked solutions, printable practice sheets and answer keys, practice exams built to the course's table of specifications, and exports for Kahoot, Blooket and Brightspace.

Coverage: Unit 1 Kinematics, Unit 2 Dynamics, Unit 3 Work and Energy, Unit 4 Waves (the 33 Knowledge indicators), plus a small prior-learning review unit drawn from Science 1206 Motion. The STSE indicators and the Integrated Skills indicators are not included: the STSE outcomes are societal and design contexts assessed in class work, and the skills are assessed in labs and projects (scope ruling, November 10, 2026).

Exam presets: each unit has a practice unit test (25 marks: 11 selected response, 14 constructed response). The mid-course review covers Units 1 and 2 at 50 marks, following the Science Table of Specifications (Kinematics 15 marks, Dynamics 35 marks). The final review covers Units 3 and 4 at 25 marks in the same senior pattern. Worked solutions follow the course's units and significant figures discipline: givens at 3 significant figures, one guard digit carried through, final answers at 3 significant figures with units.

Part of the CDLI review apps collection. Status: draft, pending QA.
